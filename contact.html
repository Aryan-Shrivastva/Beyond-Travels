SOME USEFUL THINGS TO KNOW

Before we
make a map.

How far ahead should we start?

For a considered route, six to twelve weeks gives more room for the stays and timings that matter. Shorter notice can still work; we will be direct about what is realistic.

Can you change an existing journey?

Absolutely. The journeys on the site are a starting shape. We can slow them down, make them more comfortable, or use them simply as a clue to the feeling you want.

Are the listed prices final?

No. They are clear planning estimates per adult based on two sharing. Dates, room choice, permits and availability determine a final quote.

Do you book international flights?

The current collection focuses on land arrangements after arrival. We will explain where flights fit into a route, but arrival flights are normally priced separately.
